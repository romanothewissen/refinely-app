# Privacy Policy for Refinely

Last updated: March 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing **Refinely** ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing AI-powered user story generation services within Jira. This Privacy Policy describes how **Smartif.ai** ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our App.

## 2. Information We Access

Our App operates as a **Forge** application on the Atlassian Cloud platform. We access the following information via the standard Atlassian APIs:

- **Jira Issue Data**: Content of Jira issues (titles, descriptions, comments) that you select for story generation.
- **User Information**: Basic details such as your Atlassian Account ID and display name to personalize your experience.
- **App Storage**: We use Forge's secure `storage` API to save tenant configuration, intermediate project states, and optional transparency or audit records when those features are enabled.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the accessed information solely to:

- Generate AI-powered user stories and backlog items based on the context of your existing Jira issues.
- Manage app settings and preferences.
- Provide technical support and improve app functionality.

## 4. Third-Party Data Processing (AI Services)

To provide the core functionality of the App, we may process your data using configured third-party AI providers:

- **Anthropic (via the Anthropic API)**
- **Google Gemini (via Google Cloud API)**
- **OpenAI (via OpenAI API)**

**Important Note**: When you trigger an AI generation, the relevant Jira issue context and prompt content needed to fulfill your request are transmitted to the active provider configured for your workspace. We do NOT use customer content to train our own models, and we rely on the enterprise API terms and data handling commitments provided by these subprocessors.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

- **Residency**: Our App is built on Atlassian's Forge infrastructure, meaning app-hosted storage remains within Atlassian's secure cloud environment and follows Forge platform behavior.
- **Retention**: We do not intentionally store persistent copies of Jira issue content outside of your Atlassian instance, except for the transient processing needed to fulfill an AI request and the data you explicitly save into Forge app storage through the App's features.

## 6. Your Data Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection laws, you may have rights regarding your personal data. Since most data processed by the App originates from your Atlassian Cloud instance, please contact your organization's Jira administrator first for access, correction, or deletion requests. If you need additional help related to App-controlled data, contact us using the address below.
