# Privacy Policy for Refinely

Last updated: March 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing **Refinely** ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing AI-powered user story generation services within Jira. This Privacy Policy describes how **AutoMano.ai** ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our App.

## 2. Information We Access

Our App operates as a **Forge** application on the Atlassian Cloud platform. We access the following information via the standard Atlassian APIs:

- **Jira Issue Data**: Content of Jira issues (titles, descriptions, comments) that you select for story generation.
- **User Information**: Basic details such as your Atlassian Account ID and display name to personalize your experience.
- **App Storage**: We use Forge's secure `storage` API to save your configuration settings and intermediate project states.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the accessed information solely to:

- Generate AI-powered user stories and backlog items based on the context of your existing Jira issues.
- Manage app settings and preferences.
- Provide technical support and improve app functionality.

## 4. Third-Party Data Processing (AI Services)

To provide the core functionality of the App, we process your data using high-performance AI models from third-party providers:

- **Google Gemini (via Google Cloud API)**
- **OpenAI (via OpenAI API)**

**Important Note**: When you trigger an AI generation, the relevant Jira issue context is transmitted to these providers. We do NOT use your data to train their models, and we adhere to their enterprise-grade API data privacy agreements.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

- **Residency**: Our App is built on Atlassian's Forge infrastructure, meaning data is stored and processed within Atlassian's secure cloud environment.
- **Retention**: We do not store persistent copies of your Jira data outside of your Atlassian instance or the Forge storage allocated to your tenant.

## 6. Your Data Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection laws, you have rights regarding your personal data. Since the data originates from your Atlassian Cloud instance, please contact your organization's Jira administrator for data access, correction, or deletion requests.
